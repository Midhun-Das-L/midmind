# Account and security

Forgot your password? Open the sign-in page, click "Reset password" and follow the
link sent to your email. Reset links expire after 1 hour.

Two-factor authentication (2FA) can be enabled under Settings > Security. We support
authenticator apps and hardware security keys. SMS codes are not supported.

To delete your account, go to Settings > Account > Delete account. Deletion is
permanent after a 14-day grace period, during which you can sign in to cancel it.
