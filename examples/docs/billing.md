# Billing and refunds

Nimbus Notes Pro costs $8 per month or $80 per year. The Team plan costs $12 per user per month.

Refund policy: annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase.
Monthly subscriptions are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time and keep Pro
features until the end of the billing period.

To request a refund, email billing@nimbusnotes.example with your order number.
Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.
